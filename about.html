WHO WE ARE

Where Creativity Meets Culture

DORKAM Designs is a fashion design company dedicated to creating beautiful, distinctive and thoughtfully designed clothing for women and girls.

Our designs draw inspiration from African culture, bold patterns, beautiful fabrics and contemporary fashion. We combine these influences to create outfits that feel stylish, confident and uniquely personal.

From African wear and women's fashion to girls' outfits, event wear and custom designs, every piece is created with attention to detail and a passion for fashion.
